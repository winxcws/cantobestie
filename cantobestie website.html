05旅程 The journey

Where a year of
hellos can take you.

學習線 · Learning Line5 stations · 你好 → 屋企
Lesson one

Your first words 你好

A greeting, your name, a real exchange in Cantonese — small, but entirely yours. Most students are surprised it happens this soon.

Month one

Everyday essentials 早晨

Greetings, numbers, ordering, thanking — the small currency of daily life, with tones that are starting to behave.

Month three

Small talk, real talk 傾偈

Taxis, markets, dim sum orders. You make small talk with locals — and start catching familiar words in the noise of the city.

Month six

Longer conversations 講故

Opinions, stories, plans. You watch Hong Kong shows and understand more than you miss. People stop switching to English.

Year one & beyond

It starts to feel like home 屋企

Cantonese stops feeling like a subject and starts feeling like a place you live. Some days, you even catch yourself thinking in it.

Everyone moves at their own pace — this is a typical path, not a promise. We’ll shape yours together.
